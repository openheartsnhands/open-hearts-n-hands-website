Who We Are

Serving our community with compassion, dignity and purpose.

Open Hearts N Hands is a nonprofit organization committed to strengthening families and improving lives throughout our community. We connect individuals and families with resources, provide seasonal assistance, create meaningful community experiences and work to ensure that those facing difficult circumstances know they are not alone.

Our Programs

Our programs are designed to meet practical needs while creating opportunities for families and individuals to thrive.

Holiday Toy Drive

Providing toys and holiday support to children and families throughout the community.

Back-to-School Assistance

Helping students begin the school year prepared with backpacks, supplies and other necessities.

Food & Essential Needs

Connecting families with food, toiletries and other everyday essentials during times of need.

Clothing Assistance

Providing access to clothing and seasonal essentials for individuals and families.

Community Events

Creating welcoming events that bring families together and strengthen community connections.

Family & Disability Support

Supporting children and adults with mental and physical disabilities and the families who care for them.
