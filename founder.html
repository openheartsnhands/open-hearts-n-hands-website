DeLauren Brewer Villareal

Founder & President, Open Hearts N Hands

DeLauren Brewer Villareal is the Founder and President of Open Hearts N Hands, a nonprofit organization dedicated to strengthening families, supporting individuals with disabilities, and providing meaningful resources to children and adults throughout the community.

Open Hearts N Hands was founded from DeLauren’s genuine passion for helping others and her belief that everyone deserves to feel supported, valued, and connected to their community.

Under her leadership, the organization has supported families through holiday toy drives, back-to-school initiatives, community events, essential-item assistance, and other outreach efforts.

DeLauren’s long-term vision is for Open Hearts N Hands to become a trusted, year-round resource where families can find assistance, encouragement, opportunities, and a sense of belonging.

“Open Hearts N Hands was created from a simple desire to help people. My vision is for this organization to become a place our community knows they can turn to throughout the year.”

— DeLauren Brewer Villareal
